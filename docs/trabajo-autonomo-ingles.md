## 1. What is ISO? Do all ISO standards follow the high-level structure?

ISO (the International Organization for Standardization) is a non-governmental body that builds consensus on standards meeting commercial and social needs. Its standards are voluntary because ISO has no authority to enforce them (normas9000, 2018).

Not all ISO standards follow the high-level structure. It applies only to ISO management system standards, such as ISO 9001, 14001, 27001 and 45001 (Governance Docs, 2026). Product or technical standards, such as the ISO/IEC 25000 series for software product quality, do not use it.

The structure, also called **Annex SL**, has ten clauses: scope, normative references, terms and definitions, context of the organization, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement (ISO 9001 Expert, 2026).

## 2. ISO standards related to the software product and process

| Standard | Focus | Brief description |
|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 12207 | Process | Software life cycle processes: acquisition, development, operation, maintenance. |
| ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 | Process | System life cycle processes. |
| ISO/IEC 33000 series (formerly 15504, SPICE) | Process | Framework for assessing process capability and maturity. |
| ISO/IEC 90003 | Process | Guidelines for applying ISO 9001 to software. |
| ISO 9001 | Process / management | Quality management system requirements. |
| ISO/IEC 25000 series (SQuaRE) | Product | Software product quality requirements and evaluation. |
| ISO/IEC 25010 | Product | Product quality model (functional suitability, performance, security, usability, etc.). |
| ISO/IEC 25012 | Product | Data quality model. |
| ISO/IEC 9126 and 14598 | Product (replaced) | Earlier quality model and evaluation, replaced by ISO/IEC 25000. |
| ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119 | Testing | Software testing: concepts, processes, documentation and techniques. |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Security | Information security management system. |
| ISO/IEC 20000 | Service | IT service management. |

> **Nota.** ISO/IEC 9126, 14598 y 25000 aparecen como base de evaluación de la calidad del software en la guía de laboratorio No. 6.

## 3. Colombian companies with ISO or CMMI certifications

Several Colombian software companies have held CMMI certifications. Reported level 3 companies include **Red Colombia S.A., MVM Ingeniería de Software S.A., Trébol Software S.A. and InterGrupo S.A.** (Medellín unit) (Google Groups, 2014). The **Ministerio de TIC** also named **Data Center, Ecoinfo and Colvista** among Colombian companies implementing CMMI (MinTIC, 2014). **Indra** renewed CMMI-DEV level 3 for its IT operations, including its Colombian production center (Indra, 2018).

> Because some of these sources date from 2006 to 2018, certifications may have expired. The current status should be confirmed on the CMMI Institute appraisal registry or on each company's website before submitting.

## 5. Relationship between certifications and software testing

Certifications such as ISO 9001 and CMMI require organizations to define, document and measure their processes, and testing is a central part of those processes.

**ISO 9001** requires monitoring, measurement, internal audit and corrective action for nonconformities (ISO 9001 Expert, 2026), which in software means test planning, defect tracking and fixing. **CMMI level 3** requires standard, documented processes across the organization (Indra, 2018), so verification and validation are done in a consistent, repeatable way.

> In short: **certifications give the framework for quality, and testing supplies the evidence that the product meets it.**
